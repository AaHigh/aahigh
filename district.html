The Tower Theatre, 815 E Olive Ave, at night. The marquee lit, the tower lit. This is the place.

The High Tower District is a real place. It’s the blocks around the Tower Theatre in Fresno — Olive Ave, Wishon, the 777. Everything here is built on those blocks: the games, the pizza, the sermons, the stories. My name goes up with the Tower because the work happens here, in the open, where anybody can come and see it.
